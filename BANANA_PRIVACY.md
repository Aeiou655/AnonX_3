# Privacy Policy for Ba Na Na FM

**Effective Date:** August 12, 2026  
**Telegram Bot:** @BaNaNanMusic_Bot  
**Service Name:** Ba Na Na FM 🇲🇲 🎧

This Privacy Policy explains how Ba Na Na FM ("the Bot", "we", "our", or "us") processes information when users interact with the Telegram bot, use music or video playback features, add the Bot to a Telegram group or channel, use Telegram Voice Chat functions, or open the associated Telegram Mini App.

## 1. Service Functions

Ba Na Na FM is a Telegram music and media service. Its functions may include music/video search, audio/video playback in Telegram Voice Chats, queue management, pause/resume, skip, stop/end playback, Voice Chat join/leave, direct streaming, adaptive media quality, prefetching, temporary caching, metadata/thumbnail handling, administrative functions, diagnostics, Mini App features and Telegram affiliate/referral functionality where available.

## 2. Telegram Information We May Process

Depending on how the Bot is used, Telegram may provide information such as:

- Telegram User ID
- Username
- First/last name or display name
- Telegram Chat ID
- Group/channel ID and name
- Message ID
- Bot commands
- Callback/button interactions
- Reply information
- Relevant group administrator/permission information

The Bot does not require a user's Telegram password for normal operation.

## 3. Messages and Commands

The Bot may process messages relevant to its functions, including `/play`, `/vplay`, playback-control commands, search keywords, song titles, artist names, media links, queue commands, settings and administrative commands.

If automatic-response, learning or moderation functionality is enabled, the Bot may process message text and Telegram identifiers necessary for those features.

## 4. Music and Video Search

When users request media, the Bot may process search queries, song names, artist names, video titles, media URLs, media/video IDs, audio/video mode, media quality and playback status.

## 5. External Media Providers

To provide playback, the Bot may communicate with third-party media services such as YouTube, Googlevideo/media-delivery infrastructure and other supported providers. Search queries, media IDs, media URLs and technical request information may be transmitted when necessary to fulfil a user's request.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 6. Direct Streaming and Voice Chat

Where technically available, Ba Na Na FM may stream media directly rather than waiting for a complete file download.

A playback request may involve searching for media, resolving a playable source, preparing a Telegram Voice Chat, starting media decoding, connecting the Bot or assistant account and sending audio/video into Telegram Voice Chat.

For Voice Chat operation, the Bot may process Chat ID, Voice Chat state, connection state, join/leave status, mute/unmute state, current media, playback mode and queue/session information.

## 7. Queue and Playback State

The Bot may maintain temporary or persistent playback state such as current track, requested track, media ID/title, requesting user's Telegram ID, Chat ID, queue position, playback mode, pause/resume state, current stream and next-track information.

## 8. Media Processing

The service may use media-processing tools such as FFmpeg or similar technologies. During playback it may temporarily process audio/video streams, codecs, formats, media URLs, decoded frames, temporary buffers and process/error information.

## 9. Caching, Prefetching and Metadata

To improve playback performance and reliability, the Bot may cache or prefetch resolved media URLs, metadata, thumbnails, temporary media files, current/next-track information and stream data.

Temporary cache entries may expire, be overwritten or be deleted automatically when no longer needed.

## 10. Database and Stored Settings

Ba Na Na FM may use databases and caches to store information required for group settings, user-related Bot settings, playback preferences, administrative configuration, queue/playback state, feature settings, cached media information and operational service state.

Persistent information is retained only while reasonably necessary for the related Bot feature or legal obligations.

## 11. Telegram Mini App

When users open the Ba Na Na FM Mini App, Telegram may supply initialization data needed to identify and authenticate the Telegram session. This may include Telegram User ID, username, display name, language information, chat/session context and Telegram Mini App initialization/authentication data.

This information may be used for authentication, Mini App functionality, personalization and security.

The Mini App does not require users to provide their Telegram account password.

## 12. Affiliate Program

Telegram may provide or display an Affiliate Program associated with Ba Na Na FM. When users participate, Telegram or relevant platform/payment infrastructure may process referral and spending-related information according to their own policies. Ba Na Na FM may receive information necessary to identify valid referral or affiliate activity where Telegram makes such data available.

Ba Na Na FM does not independently request users' banking passwords or payment PINs.

## 13. Administrative Access

Certain functions may be restricted to the Bot owner, privileged/sudo users, group administrators or authorized operators. Such users may access limited technical information where reasonably necessary to maintain the Bot, diagnose errors, manage groups, prevent abuse and protect infrastructure.

## 14. Logs and Diagnostics

The Bot may generate technical logs containing information such as date/time, Chat ID, User ID where necessary, commands, search queries, media IDs, playback state, Voice Chat state, request/trace identifiers, cache status, media-source status, performance timings and error information.

These logs are used for debugging, reliability, security, abuse prevention, service maintenance and performance improvement.

## 15. Information We Do Not Intentionally Request

For normal use, Ba Na Na FM does not require users to provide Telegram passwords, two-factor authentication passwords, banking passwords, payment PINs, private API keys, Telegram session files or email passwords.

Users should never send sensitive credentials to the Bot.

## 16. Data Sharing

Ba Na Na FM does **not sell users' personal information** to advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be processed or shared only when reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, deliver requested media, provide infrastructure, prevent abuse, protect the service, or comply with valid legal obligations.

## 17. Data Retention

Temporary playback, stream, cache and process data may be removed automatically after playback, expiration, cleanup or server restart.

Persistent settings or configuration may remain while the related feature is active or until deleted. Diagnostic logs may be retained for a reasonable period required for security, diagnostics and maintenance.

## 18. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of personal information controlled by Ba Na Na FM. Where technically possible and legally permitted, information that is no longer required will be deleted.

Removing the Bot from a group stops future Bot interaction in that group but may not immediately remove historical technical logs or previously stored settings.

## 19. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect Bot tokens, Telegram session credentials, assistant credentials, server credentials, API credentials, databases and other operational information.

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## 20. Abuse Prevention

The Bot may process technical information and Telegram identifiers where reasonably necessary to prevent spam, detect abuse, enforce resource limits, investigate malicious activity and protect Bot infrastructure.

## 21. Children's Privacy

Ba Na Na FM does not intentionally collect unnecessary sensitive personal information from children. Users must comply with Telegram's age requirements and applicable law.

## 22. Telegram's Role

Ba Na Na FM is an independent third-party Telegram Bot and is not Telegram Messenger Inc. Telegram separately processes user information under Telegram's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

This Privacy Policy applies only to processing performed by Ba Na Na FM and its service infrastructure.

## 23. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Bot functions, Mini App behavior, storage practices, third-party integrations or legal/platform requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

## 24. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or deletion requests related to Ba Na Na FM, contact the service operator through the official Telegram contact associated with the Bot.
