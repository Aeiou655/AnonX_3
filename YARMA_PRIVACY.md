# Privacy Policy for YarMa Music

**Bot:** YarMa Music  
**Telegram:** @yarmamusic_bot  
**Last Updated:** August 13, 2026

## Information We Process

YarMa Music may process basic Telegram information required to provide its features, including:

- Telegram User ID
- Chat or Group ID
- Username and display name
- Bot commands and music search requests
- Voice chat and playback-related information

## How We Use Information

This information is used only to:

- Process bot commands
- Search and play requested music or videos
- Manage music queues and voice chat playback
- Maintain bot functionality, security, and performance
- Prevent abuse and misuse of the service

## Data Storage

YarMa Music may temporarily store technical information, settings, queues, cached media information, and usage data necessary for the bot to operate.

We do not intentionally collect passwords, payment card information, or other sensitive personal information.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Some requests may be processed through third-party services required for music search, media streaming, hosting, or Telegram functionality. Those services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

## Groups and Channels

When YarMa Music is added to a group or channel, it may process messages, commands, user IDs, chat IDs, and other information necessary to perform the requested bot functions.

## Data Retention

Temporary and cached information may be automatically deleted when it is no longer required for operation, maintenance, or security.

## User Control

Users may stop using the bot at any time or remove YarMa Music from a group or channel.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the owner or administrator of **@yarmamusic_bot** through Telegram.
